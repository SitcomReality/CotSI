# BIOMES:

## Unfinished Lands: where reality was being rewritten by a god that died in the divine war.
* Strange mobs - featureless things that were never completed and phantoms made of unrealized ideas.
* Changes can happen when nobody is looking.
* ARCHIVE combat penalty, REVERIE combat bonus 
* Weather can be randomly different to the global weather, including a unique weather condition (or two) that only occurs in this biome

## Mourning Marsh: a cool land soaked in the tears of a mourning god
* The weather here always has rain
* VERDANT combat bonus, HEARTH combat penalty

## Painforest: rainforest imbued with the echoes of a dying god's screams
* Unique flora mobs and some flora that's normally neutral or good can turn into a hostile mob
* CRUCIBLE and VERDANT combat bonus

## Brass Grave: a piece of a divine war machine embedded in the world
* Conceptually/thematically, this would be a piece of something from a cosmic scale, like a fragment of a war machine that was the size of the whole planet.
* Much of the terrain in the biome is unique to this biome: brass (and maybe others?)
* Also unique features, such as whatever greebles a divine construct might have had? Like a wire protruding from the ground (which would be like the size of a faction base, because it's a cosmic scale wire)
* Unique mob/s: remnants of the war machine's consciousness wander its dead skin
* VERDANT combat penalty on brass terrain

## Frigid Silence: a place where there's no sound 
* moist 0.22-0.60 ?
* MASQUE & HOLLOW combat bonus, HEARTH & REVERIE combat penalty
